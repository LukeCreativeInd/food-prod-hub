# EveryBatch Review Register

## Purpose

Reviews are structured stakeholder/evidence checkpoints. They do not consume Task numbers and are not automatically technical gates. Development can continue before and after a Review. Findings may leave the horizon unchanged or justify acceptance changes, backlog additions, an urgent lettered subtask, architecture correction or horizon replan with Luke's approval.

## Required Review Record

Every Review records: number, title, status, scheduled/completed date, known participants or roles, purpose, assumptions tested, affected modules/workflows, findings, decisions, contradictions, new requirements, candidate-backlog additions, active-horizon impact, follow-up and documents changed. Unknown information remains `TBD`; findings are never fabricated.

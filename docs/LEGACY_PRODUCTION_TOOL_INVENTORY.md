## Retirement Governance

Every item remains active until its evidence is documented, its replacement is implemented, parallel-run results are accepted, staff validate real production days, fallback/support exist and Luke approves retirement. Archiving a tool for reference must preserve the source/configuration and representative outputs needed to explain historical production decisions.

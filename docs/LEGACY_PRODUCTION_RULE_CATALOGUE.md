## High-Risk Findings

The most dangerous behaviours are exact-title silent exclusion, loss of store/order/date provenance, manual unlinked adjustments, bundle handling that assumes upstream child expansion, inconsistent active comments/calculations, duplicate stale modules and hard-coded formula/method/presentation concepts with no version history.

## Validation Ownership

- Luke: store relationships, roadmap, product/mapping acceptance, decommission authority.
- Clean Eats production leadership: current quantities, yields, batch/rounding rules, area ownership, pack distribution and instruction content.
- QA leadership: HACCP header, shelf-life/use-by and check requirements.
- Warehouse/prepack/kitchen staff: task practicality and physical transfer/staging requirements.
- EveryBatch architecture: source preservation, provider-neutral boundaries, versioning, tenant/facility safety and parity automation.
